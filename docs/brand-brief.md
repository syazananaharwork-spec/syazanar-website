# Syazanar — Brand brief

Live at syazanar.vercel.app

## Industry

Website design, build, and systems for Malaysian SMEs.

## Primary offer

Done-for-you custom websites that turn visitors into enquiries.
Three fixed tiers plus custom work:

- **Starter** — 1-3 pages, RM2,250
- **Growth** — 5-8 pages + WhatsApp enquiry flow, RM6,000
- **Premium** — 10+ pages + multilingual, RM12,000
- **Custom** — system integration, booking/payment/CRM, quoted

## Target audience

Malaysian SME owners: clinics, F&B, retail, and professional
services. Businesses that either have no website, or have a
website that generates zero enquiries. B2C is included. This is
NOT the same buyer as the LinkedIn ghostwriting client.

## Primary CTA

Request a quote: WhatsApp message or enquiry form that lands
directly with Sya.

## Secondary CTA

View portfolio: live client sites, concept demos, and case pages
that show the tier differences.

## Brand personality

Calm, precise, restrained, fast, honest. Short sentences. No hype,
no emoji in body copy, no em dashes. A website is infrastructure,
not fireworks. The brand speaks to a busy owner as someone who
will not waste their time.

That restraint carries into how the site is built, not just how
it reads. One neutral base carries the page: the existing
dark/metal palette. Amber shows up thin and deliberate, a link, a
button, a hover state, never a flood. Headlines get tight,
confident tracking; small labels get wide, deliberate tracking.
Nothing moves without a reason: state changes, like a header going
solid on scroll, happen decisively, not as a lingering fade. No
splash screen, no loading gate. The page paints as soon as it's
ready, because making a busy owner wait is the one thing this
brand doesn't do.

## Required pages

- Home
- Work (portfolio)
- Services (tier comparison)
- About
- Contact
